# Kotoba Foundation and Awai Network LLC

Decision: 2026-10-13 (Asia/Tokyo), by the project owner.

## Responsibility

| Scope | Responsible organization |
| --- | --- |
| Kotoba Lang OSS, open specifications, shared open-source libraries and decentralized protocols | Kotoba Foundation |
| OSS and decentralized components of the wider Kotoba ecosystem | Kotoba Foundation |
| Centralized service at kotoba.cloud | Awai Network LLC |
| Centralized service at murakumo.cloud | Awai Network LLC |
| Centralized service at itonami.cloud | Awai Network LLC |
| Centralized service at aozora.app | Awai Network LLC |

Kotoba Foundation stewards OSS development, maintenance and governance, and
the open specifications and decentralized parts of the ecosystem.
Awai Network LLC operates the four hosted services, including their centralized
infrastructure, service contracts, billing, customer support and service-specific
data handling. Existing company disclosures use **AWAI Network, L.L.C.**;
**Awai Network LLC** is the organization name used in this governance policy.

The boundary follows the component's role. OSS and decentralized protocols
remain under Kotoba Foundation stewardship when used by an Awai-operated
service. A hosted gateway, control plane or managed deployment is an Awai
service even when its implementation is open source. Foundation stewardship
does not make independent participants' nodes Foundation-operated services.

## 日本語

Kotoba Lang の OSS、およびエコシステムの OSS・分散型部分の運営・保守・
ガバナンスを **Kotoba Foundation** が担う。**kotoba.cloud、murakumo.cloud、
itonami.cloud、aozora.app** の中央集権的なサービス運営は **Awai Network LLC**
に集約する。サービス運営にはホスティング、契約、課金、サポート、および
サービス固有のデータ取扱いを含む。

サービスが利用する OSS・分散型プロトコルと、サービスそのものの運営主体を
区別する。独立した参加者のノードの運営責任は、その参加者にある。

## Implementation boundary

This records the organizational responsibility decision. It does not establish
Foundation incorporation or jurisdiction, transfer contributor copyright,
change an OSS license, execute an asset or contract assignment, or prove that
production disclosures and provider accounts have been migrated. Those facts
require their own records. Existing legal names, registration details and
payment-collection arrangements remain evidence for those specific functions.

Workspace decision: [shared governance policy](https://github.com/com-junkawasaki/root/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).
